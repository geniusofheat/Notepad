Welcome to Notepad

Keep all your notes in lists, formatted the way you like, and open them on any device.

What you can do

Lists and notes

Make a list for each subject, then add as many notes to it as you need.

Formatting

Bold, italic, underline, alignment, indents, fonts, colors, highlights, bullet and numbered lists, and checkboxes.

Voice and photos

Speak your note instead of typing it, and add photos to any note.

Every device, offline too

Sign in with Google on any device to see the same notes. The app still works without internet and saves your changes when you are back online.

Private to you

Each account keeps its own notes, so no one else sees yours.
